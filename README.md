# A “Frontier” OSR / Shadowdark Campaign Setting
Based on the idea of a False Hydra from [Goblin Punch](https://goblinpunch.blogspot.com/2014/09/false-hydra.html).

A scenic valley, rich in natural resources and history, lays nestled in between steep mountains and an unexplored and wild ocean.
Twenty years ago, a False Hydra ravaged its entirety.
Everyone not eaten or enthralled by the Hydra fled to the sea or the single mountain pass connecting the region.
Some towns were purposely burned down, some were abandoned before the Hydra even came.
Bandits prayed upon the refugees, carrying all their worldly possessions with them.
Mines, forests and fields lay empty and barren.

In the paranoia and the confusion, only one fact was certain:
The Hydra ate people to stay alive.
So the pass was magically sealed and a large harbor fortress erected.
With the only two known connections closely guarded, time was left to run its course.

Many left.
The new border region, supported mostly by the rich lands now sealed off, could not support everyone.
Some stayed, to wait, to guard the pass, sometimes to find another way back in.
