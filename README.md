# A “Frontier” OSR / Shadowdark Campaign Setting
Based on the idea of the False Hydra from [Goblin Punch](https://goblinpunch.blogspot.com/2014/09/false-hydra.html) (big spoilers!).
The campaign begins twenty years after the Hydra depopulated an entire valley, which was sealed off after the survivors fled the region.
With the seal lifted, everything is up for grabs:
Land, natural resources, the treasures left behind in the panic.
All the while the thread of the Hydra's return lingers in the background,
being pushed to the background by the dangers of a land untouched by humans for such a long time.

It fuses elements of a post-apocalyptic ~~waste~~ “lush” land with those of a Wild West frontier setting.
